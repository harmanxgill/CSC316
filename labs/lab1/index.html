Maturity trends diverged across Netflix genres

Each cell is the share of titles rated Mature (R, NC-17, TV-MA) among all rated titles Netflix added that year. Darker means a bigger mature share. From 2017 on, TV genres grew more mature (TV Dramas 32% to 63%, International TV Shows 28% to 60%) while film genres grew less mature (Thrillers 85% to 62%, Comedies 48% to 38%).

Method

Maturity groups. Kids: TV-Y, TV-Y7, TV-Y7-FV. Family: G, TV-G, PG, TV-PG. Teen: PG-13, TV-14. Mature: R, NC-17, TV-MA. Each cell is the Mature count divided by the total across all four groups for that genre and year.

Multi-genre counting. listed_in holds a comma-separated list, so a title counts once in every genre it lists. A title tagged “Dramas, Thrillers” appears in both rows. Rows overlap and do not add up to the catalog, and each row has its own denominator. The 12 genres shown cover genre-title pairings out of genres in the data.

Excluded.

Year comes from date_added, the date Netflix added the title, not release_year, so this tracks what Netflix acquired rather than what studios produced. Overall pools all seven years for that genre.
